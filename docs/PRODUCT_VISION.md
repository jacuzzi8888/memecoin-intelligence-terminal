# Product Vision

## Mission

Build the most intelligent, transparent, and actionable memecoin intelligence platform on Solana, giving traders an asymmetric information advantage through wallet forensics, token analysis, and real-time signal detection.

## Core Problem

Memecoin trading on Solana is dominated by:
- **Information asymmetry**: A small group of sophisticated wallets consistently profits while retail traders lose
- **Noise overload**: Thousands of tokens launch daily; most are noise, scams, or rugs
- **Reactive trading**: Traders see opportunities after the move, not before
- **No explainability**: Existing tools give scores without context; traders cannot trust or learn from them

## Solution

A unified intelligence platform that:

1. **Discovers alpha** by analyzing historical winners and extracting the wallets that profited
2. **Filters noise** by identifying and excluding bots, bundlers, farms, and insiders
3. **Backtests survivors** to prove edge before monitoring them
4. **Monitors in real-time** to detect coordinated entries into fresh tokens
5. **Analyzes the token** itself for risk, liquidity, deployer history, and launch fingerprints
6. **Scores explainably** so traders understand why a signal fires
7. **Delivers alerts** through the trader's preferred channel
8. **Enables action** through a non-custodial trading terminal

## Product Surfaces

### Web Application
Dense, professional interface for research, monitoring, and trading. Built for speed and clarity under decision pressure.

### Telegram Bot
Primary mobile interface for alerts, scanning, and quick trade execution. Deep-linked to web for complex analysis.

### Notification System
Multi-channel delivery (Telegram, Discord, web push, email) with consistent formatting and deep links.

## Guiding Principles

1. **Transparency over opacity**: Every score includes its factors, confidence, and data freshness
2. **Wallet-first intelligence**: The edge comes from understanding wallets, not just tokens
3. **Provider-agnostic architecture**: Swap data providers without rewriting the product
4. **Versioned intelligence**: All scores and strategies are versioned for reproducibility
5. **Safety by default**: No live trading without explicit user approval; no private keys ever stored
6. **Progressive complexity**: Start with what works; add complexity only when justified by data

## Long-Term Vision

The platform will evolve into a complete memecoin trading intelligence system with:
- Historical backtesting and strategy optimization
- Wallet clustering and funding relationship detection
- Social sentiment integration
- Advanced graph exploration
- Custom strategy builder
- Multi-chain expansion

## Success Metrics

- **Signal accuracy**: Percentage of alerts that lead to profitable outcomes
- **False positive rate**: Percentage of alerts that do not lead to action
- **Time-to-alert**: Time from token launch to signal delivery
- **User retention**: Daily active users / total users
- **Trade execution rate**: Percentage of alerts that lead to attempted trades
